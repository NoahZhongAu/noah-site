Which one, and why

A, crossfade + rise is what the PRD specifies. Calm, cheap, never wrong. It is the baseline the others are measured against.

B, focus pull is the "screen focuses on this experience" feeling you described. The outgoing era blurs and dims, the incoming one sharpens, and the image keeps pushing in very slowly while you read. It costs a blur filter on two layers during the transition, which is fine on desktop and needs testing on a mid-range phone.

C, slide + parallax feels like turning a page. The backdrop moves less than the text, so the scene has depth. Cheap, all transforms.

D, cinematic wipe is the most theatrical. Letterbox bars appear for the beat of the transition and the new era wipes up from the bottom. Strong, but loud; use it only if the rest of the site is quiet.

My pick is B. It matches the illustrated, cinematic hero, the "focus" metaphor fits a résumé, and it stays inside the PRD's rule of animating only transform, opacity and filter.
